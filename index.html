Before the plumber arrives

Emergency steps that limit the damage

Shut the water off

Turn the main valve to closed where the line enters the house (clockwise for a wheel handle, a quarter turn for a lever), or close the shutoff under the fixture that is leaking.

Keep power away from water

If water is near outlets, lights or the panel, stay out of the room and shut off the breaker only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.

Smell gas? Leave first

Walk outside, then call your gas utility and 911. Do not flip switches or use a flame on the way out.
