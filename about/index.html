Newark 24/7 Plumbing is a licensed and insured plumbing company based at 800 Creek View Road in Newark, DE. We take emergency calls around the clock and book planned work by appointment, for homes and small businesses in Newark and the towns around it.

The business is built around the phone. There is no web form to fill in and no waiting for an email reply. You call, you describe the problem, and a licensed plumber is sent to your address.

How we work

When you call, we ask where you are, what is happening and what you have already shut off. That tells us how urgent the job is and what to bring. On site, the plumber finds the cause, explains the options in plain language and gives you a clear price before work starts.

We offer same-day appointments when available for work that is not an emergency. We do not promise an arrival time, because a burst pipe across town can change the schedule, and we would rather tell you that than pretend otherwise.

Homes, rentals and small businesses

Most calls come from homeowners, but landlords, property managers and small businesses call too. Tell us whether you own the building or manage it, who can give access and whether tenants are affected. Small businesses can call at any hour, since the line is open around the clock.

Before you call

If water is escaping, close the nearest shutoff valve or the main valve where the line enters the house. If water is near outlets, lights or the electrical panel, stay out of the room and shut off the breaker only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.

A gas smell comes first: step outside with everyone, call the gas utility and 911, and phone us once you are clear of the building. These steps limit damage while a plumber is on the way, and the dispatcher can walk you through them on the phone.

Emergencies we handle

The 24/7 line covers burst and frozen pipes, sewer backups, clogged drains, failed water heaters, leaking toilets and faucets, jammed disposals, failed sump pumps and no-heat boiler calls. Each of those has a service page that lists the first steps to take while you wait.

Licensed and insured

The company is licensed and insured, which matters most when something goes wrong mid-job. If you want to check a license yourself, the plumber can give you the details and you can look them up on the state site before work starts. Plumbing in Delaware is licensed work, and the state keeps a public license lookup. Gas piping and heating work have their own licensing rules, and we point those out on the pages where they apply.
