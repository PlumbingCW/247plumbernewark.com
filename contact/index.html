Call now

The fastest way to reach a plumber is to call (410) 378-7601. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, including nights, weekends and holidays. We do not use a web form, so there is nothing to wait on.

For non-urgent questions you can email contact@247plumbernewark.com. Email is not monitored for emergencies, so if water is spreading or sewage is backing up, call.

Where we are

Newark 24/7 Plumbing is at 800 Creek View Road, Newark, DE 19711. Plumbers are dispatched from here to your address. The map below shows our location, and the service area page explains the towns we reach.

What to have ready when you call

  • The address where the plumber is needed, including the town and ZIP code.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you have found and closed a shutoff valve.
  • Whether water is near outlets, lights or the electrical panel.
  • A good number to reach you if the call drops.

Before you call

Before you dial, stop the water if you can by turning the closest fixture valve or the main valve at the foundation. A gas odor changes the order of things: get everyone outside, phone the gas company and 911 from there, and reach us afterward about the pipes.

Calls are answered around the clock, holidays included.
